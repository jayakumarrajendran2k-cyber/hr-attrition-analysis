# HR Attrition & Performance Analysis

Analysis of employee attrition to find why employees leave and what the company can do to reduce it.

## Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, HTML dashboard

## Dataset
Synthetic practice dataset of 1,470 employees (age, department, income, overtime, satisfaction, tenure, performance, attrition).

## Key Insights
- Overtime employees leave at 35.4% vs 12.1% without overtime
- Sales has the highest attrition (24.7%)
- Employees with 0-2 years tenure leave at 20.9%
- Lowest income band leaves at 22.5% vs 12.5% in the highest band
- Leavers and stayers have similar performance ratings
